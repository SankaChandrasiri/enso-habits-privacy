Ensō Habits – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Ensō Habits is developed by Sanka Chandrasiri.

What the app stores

The habits you create (including their steps), the days you mark as done, your weekly strength checks, your weekly reviews and your settings. This data is stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage, in a separate file for each account that uses the app.

Signing in

You can sign in with Google or continue without an account. If you sign in with Google, the app reads your name and email address from Google through Android's Credential Manager. They are used only on your phone, to show who is signed in and to keep each person's habits separate. They are never sent to the developer or to any server.

What the developer collects

Nothing. The app has no analytics, no advertising and no server of its own. The developer never receives your habits, your name or your email address.

Backups

If Android backup is turned on in your phone settings, Android may include the app's data in your personal Google backup. That backup is managed by Google under your Google account, not by the developer. You can also export a backup file yourself from Profile; that file goes wherever you choose to save it.

Notifications

If you turn on a habit reminder or the daily check-in reminder, the app asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled on the phone itself.

App lock

If you turn on app lock, Android's own unlock screen checks your fingerprint, face or screen lock and tells the app only whether it succeeded. The app never sees or stores your fingerprint, face data, PIN or pattern.

Delete your data

You can delete all of your data at any time, without contacting anyone:

  1. Open Ensō Habits and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete my data and sign out, then Delete.

This removes every habit, log, review and setting for that account from the phone and signs you out. Uninstalling the app also removes all of its data from the phone. To remove the app's data from your Google backup, open your phone's Settings → Google → Backup (or Google Drive → Backups) and delete the Ensō Habits backup. Because the developer stores no data, there is nothing else to delete; if you have questions, write to the contact email on the app's Google Play page.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer at the email address shown on the app's Google Play listing.
